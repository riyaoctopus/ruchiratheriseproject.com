Project Overview

A Landmark Address,
Rooted at Budigere Cross.

The Rise by Ruchira is a RERA-registered, BDA-approved luxury high-rise residential project on a 4-acre site at Budigere Cross, East Bengaluru. Two towers — 42 and 34 storeys — rise to 156 metres and are joined by an amenity-rich sky bridge, housing 342 low-density residences with 11 ft floor heights, cross-ventilation and 70% open, landscaped space.

With only 4–6 homes per floor and 86 homes per acre, The Rise is built for privacy and space — backed by Ruchira Projects' 13+ year track record of on-time delivery in East Bengaluru.
